About

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University (B.Eng., 2023–2027). My research concerns robotic manipulation: enabling robot arms to operate competently in unstructured human environments, where object poses, scene layout, and task specifications are not known in advance.

My work spans both system integration and policy learning. As a member of the Tinker Robot Team (Future Robotics Club, Tsinghua University), I develop domestic service robots for the RoboCup@Home league, where a single platform must perceive a cluttered scene, interpret a spoken instruction, and execute the navigation and manipulation it entails under competition conditions. I additionally maintain a ROS 2 software stack for a real dual-arm platform, covering calibration, perception, and motion planning.

This systems experience motivates my current research direction. Rather than composing perception, planning, and control as separately engineered stages, I study vision-language-action models and world models — policies that map observations and instructions directly to actions while maintaining an internal prediction of how those actions alter the scene. Two questions particularly interest me: how much visual input a policy genuinely requires at each control step, and whether explicit prediction of the future justifies its inference cost.

I am currently seeking research and internship opportunities in robot learning and embodied artificial intelligence, and welcome enquiries regarding collaboration.

Research Interests

  • Robotic manipulation — grasping, placement, and dual-arm coordination in cluttered environments with uncertain object state.
  • Vision-language-action models — policies conditioned jointly on visual observation and natural-language instruction, and their generalisation to unseen objects and task descriptions.
  • World models for robot learning — learned environment dynamics that permit planning over imagined rollouts, and the conditions under which such imagination is worth its computational cost.
  • Efficient embodied inference — reducing the perception and denoising budget of learned policies without sacrificing closed-loop task success.

Research Experience

Temporal redundancy in visuomotor policies

Consecutive camera observations are highly correlated, and a robot has privileged knowledge of the action it has just executed. I investigate predicting the next visual latent from the current latent, the executed action, and proprioceptive state, propagating this belief recursively and correcting it only when a genuine image is encoded. The predicted representation conditions a diffusion policy directly. Closed-loop performance is characterised as a success rate against the proportion of control steps at which the visual encoder is invoked, quantifying how much perception may be omitted before manipulation performance degrades.

Frozen DINOv2 representations · 1D U-Net diffusion policy · LIBERO benchmark in MuJoCo · component-level latency profiling

World-action models for deformable object manipulation

I work on models that jointly denoise video and action sequences within a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture trained by flow matching, extending an existing open-source world-action model. My contributions concern a training variant that exploits privileged simulator state, together with the supporting data pipeline: appearance augmentation and asset construction for garment and cloth-folding tasks, in which object configuration is high-dimensional and only partially observable.

Separate video and action experts · flow matching · multi-GPU DeepSpeed ZeRO training · LIBERO and RoboTwin benchmarks

Dual-arm manipulation platform

I develop and maintain the ROS 2 software stack for a dual-arm lifting platform comprising two 6-DoF arms with wrist-mounted depth cameras on a motorised column. The work covers driver bring-up across both arm controllers, hand–eye calibration, perception and vision nodes, motion planning, and task-level controllers for pick-and-place routines on physical hardware.

ROS 2 Jazzy · MoveIt 2 · RealSense depth cameras · hand–eye calibration · colcon
